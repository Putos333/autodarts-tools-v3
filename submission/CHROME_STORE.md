```
🎯 Tools for Autodarts turns play.autodarts.io into the immersive dart-match
experience it deserves.

━━━ THE HIGHLIGHTS ━━━

🎙️ VOICE CALLER
25+ voice packs (English + German), CSV/ZIP-import for custom voices,

12 CC0-licensed samples routed through venue-specific audio filters —

opponents with realistic averages (28–115), sponsor system, trophies.

🎪 6 VENUES with authentic reverb + crowd character:
Alexandra Palace · Winter Gardens · Butlin's Minehead · Utilita Arena
Newcastle · TV Studio · Local Pub. Smart auto-selection based on your
skill level and tournament.

🤖 AI COMMENTATOR (opt-in)
Live English/German commentary during matches using Claude Sonnet 4.5.

📊 ELO LADDER + MARATHON LEADERBOARD
Global rankings for players and speedrun tournaments.

📱 1-CLICK SOCIAL SHARE
Automatic post-match share cards for Twitter/Reddit/Facebook.

📹 FACE-TO-FACE WEBRTC
Peer-to-peer video chat with your dart buddies during online matches.

🛡️ RELIABILITY (v2.9.87)
Board-disconnect detection, Discord 429 retry logic, settings backup/
restore, career CSV export, per-match logging.

🎨 UNLIMITED CUSTOMIZATION
Bot renamer, custom animations, screenshot export, walk-on songs,
```
